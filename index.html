BIOGRAPHY

The Beginning: The Maze

A group of teenagers wake up inside a mysterious place called the Glade, surrounded by massive walls. With no memory of their past, they must learn to survive and understand the Maze.

The Escape

Led by Thomas, the group begins to uncover secrets about the Maze. Facing dangerous creatures and deadly challenges, they work together to finally escape.

The Outside World

After escaping, they discover the world is destroyed by a deadly virus. Their journey continues as they fight for survival and search for truth.

About

This website is dedicated to the Maze Runner series, a dystopian story about survival, courage, and teamwork. It follows a group of teenagers trapped in a mysterious maze with no memory of their past.

Through this platform, we explore the journey of the characters and the challenges they face as they try to escape and uncover the truth.
